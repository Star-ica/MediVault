Why MediVault

Privacy, consent and a verifiable audit trail.

🔐

Patient-owned

Records belong to your wallet. Nobody sees them without your consent.

🏥

Verified hospitals

Only hospitals approved by the network admin can add records.

⏱️

Time-limited access

Grant a doctor access for 1 to 365 days. It expires automatically.

🚫

Instant revoke

Withdraw a doctor’s access with a single transaction.

🧾

Audit trail

Every record, grant and revoke is timestamped on-chain.

📎

Files stay off-chain

Only an encrypted file’s hash or IPFS CID is stored, never the file.

How it works

Three roles, one shared source of truth.

1

Hospital adds a record

After a visit, an approved hospital logs the record against your wallet address.

2

You grant access

Share your address with a new doctor and grant them access for a set time.

3

Doctor reads the history

The doctor enters your address to view the records you’ve shared.
